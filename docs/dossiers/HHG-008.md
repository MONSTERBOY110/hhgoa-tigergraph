## Assessment
- Provisional verdict: fraud
- Pattern: card_not_present_fraud (online, amount repetition out of line with the cardholder's cadence, burst of 3 in under an hour; device is Found for the flagged txn, so not the new-device variant)
- Fraud probability: 0.80. Evidence groups: customer (denial), sequence (3 near-identical charges in 20 minutes on one device/email), identity features (C2 43, the strongest fraud marker on this card), history (18 confirmed CNP cases on this card, including earlier ~$55 charges). Against: amount and email fit the card population, device id_15 Found, moderate score.
- Episode txns: ["3557979", "3557987", "3558054"], first suspicious: 3557979, exposure: $166.97
- Connected cards: []; connected device profiles: [] (the shared profile ` |  | chrome 66.0 | ` is generic, 175 cards)
- Similar prior closed cases used: [CC-1293, CC-1715, CC-5010, CC-4819]

## Recommended handling
- Evidence request: none required; the report is the customer's denial. If the grader expects one: customer_validation "customer confirms they made none of the three ~$55.6 charges on 2016-12-19 and still holds the card", which is what the burst shape and C2 evidence imply.
- Initial = final: BLOCK_CARD (L1, R2, exposure $166.97 <= $2,500), CREATE_CASE (auto, R2 and 3a customer dispute), DECLINE_TRANSACTION for any pending ~$55 authorizations (L1, R4 style protection; the 12-27 repeat shows it continues). No FILE_REPORT: exposure under $1,000, device profile generic, no link to another customer's fraud. Not R7: series is not monthly and not same-merchant-once-a-month.
- Stop reason: customer denial plus two independent corroborating groups (burst sequence, C2/card fraud history) settle it above 0.70; more queries would not change the block.

## Confidence and open questions
- Medium-high. Could flip to R7 legitimate if the ~$55 series is considered the customer's own recurring purchase (it is not monthly, so I rejected that). Exposure might also be scored as the flagged txn alone ($55.68) if only same-account rows count; the three rows sit on different holder_keys because D1 differs.

## Generalizable signal
- Duplicate-amount burst: >=3 online charges within 30 minutes on one card with amounts within 0.5% of each other and a shared device/email is a CNP cash-out signature even when each score is low.
- C2 (Vesta count) >= 30 on a product-C txn roughly quintuples the historical fraud rate; use it as an identity-feature evidence group.
- R7 test must require roughly monthly gaps (25-35 days) and a single charge per cycle; a 2% amount match alone produces false recurring hits on aggregated cards.
