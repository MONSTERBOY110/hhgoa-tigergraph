## Generalizable signal
- Device card-cycling detector: for any device_key with popularity <= 10, if it appears on >= 3 distinct cards within 72h and at least two of those txns (on different cards) have amounts within 1% of each other, flag every card on that device as a probable compromise and link them (R6). Weight it higher when the target cards have prior confirmed fraud.
