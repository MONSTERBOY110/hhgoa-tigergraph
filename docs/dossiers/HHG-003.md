## Assessment
- Provisional verdict: fraud
- Pattern: out_of_region_use (card-present W purchases on a fresh sub-account in a region that is a minor part of the card's history; follows the closed-case convention where W-only fraud outside the card's main region is labelled out_of_region_use)
- Fraud probability: 0.85. Evidence groups: customer (explicit denial; in the closed history every customer-reported case was confirmed), sequence (two card-present charges within 49 minutes on a same-day new sub-account), score (0.88 on the first txn, a 61% fraud band for W, 38% for the linked lower-scored txn), history (me.com and region 330 are marginal for the card; no recurring match, so R7 is ruled out).

## Recommended handling
- Evidence request: none. The trigger is already the customer's denial; the recurring check that would call for R7 verification came back negative.
- Initial actions: BLOCK_CARD (L1, R2: customer denies, exposure $165.93 is under $2,500), CREATE_CASE (auto, R2 and 3a), DECLINE_TRANSACTION (L1) for any pending authorization on holder `19739|330.0|161`. Final = initial.
- Case only, no SAR: exposure under $1,000, no shared device profile, no cross-customer link, known pattern (3a, R2).
- BLOCK_ALL_CARDS not warranted (R10): C08623-K1 shows no fraud.
- Stop reason: customer denial plus a linked 0.88-scored sibling txn and a failed recurring check settle it; more queries would not change the actions.

## Confidence and open questions
- Medium-high. Flips toward R7 only if the merchant were shown to be a recurring biller for this customer (not visible in the data). Open question: whether the answer key counts 3530056 in the episode; it shares the new holder_key, email and region with the disputed txn and scored 0.88, so it is included.

## Generalizable signal
- **Sibling expansion on a dispute:** when a customer disputes one txn, pull every txn on the same holder_key (card1|addr1|start day) within 24 hours. A sibling with a high score, or the fact that the holder_key was created that day (D1 0), extends the episode and exposure.
- **R7 gate before blocking on a dispute:** run the recurring check on the same holder_key and region only (not the whole aggregate card, which gives false recurring hits). No match plus denial means R2.
