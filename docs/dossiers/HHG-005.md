## Generalizable signal
- **"New device" is only a signal relative to the card's own base rate.** Compute the share of the card's prior online txns with id_15 New and D1 0. If the card routinely shops online from fresh devices (here nearly every R/H txn), a New flag adds no evidence; weight it only when the card normally reuses Found devices.

A popular iPad profile seen on 5 cards over 3 weeks in 5 regions is not a ring.
