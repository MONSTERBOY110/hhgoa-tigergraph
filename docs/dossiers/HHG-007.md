## Assessment
- Provisional verdict: fraud (moderate confidence)
- Pattern: account_takeover (follows the account's most recent confirmed precedents CC-5521 and CC-2834; the account's credentials/card data stay compromised after a claimed reissue). Alternative: out_of_region_use per CC-2565, rejected because 264 is the home region.
- Fraud probability: 0.78. Evidence groups: history (same account in 3 confirmed-fraud cases, most recent 5 weeks earlier), score (0.87, and base rate 93% for high scores on prior-fraud holders), sequence (a second high-score hit on the same account 2 days earlier, 3508487). Against: amount, product and region fully consistent with the account; no device or network evidence.
- Episode txns: ["3508487", "3514948"], first suspicious: 3508487, exposure: $228.88. (Wider reading if the key uses the whole account since the last case: add 3428558 and 3444972, exposure $372.85; post-alert txns 3538910 onward would be monitored, not counted.)

## Recommended handling
- Evidence request: customer_validation after the history step. Assumed reply: customer does not recognise 3514948 or 3508487 (implied by the account having been confirmed compromised three times and the model scoring it at the level that was 93% fraud historically for such accounts).
- Initial: VERIFY_WITH_CUSTOMER (auto, R1: evidence is history plus score, no behavioural anomaly), MONITOR_CARD (auto), CREATE_CASE (auto, 3a: p >= 0.30 and evidence requested).
- Final (after denial): BLOCK_CARD (L1, R2, exposure $228.88 <= $2,500; and ensure a real reissue this time, with a new card number), CREATE_CASE (auto, R2). No FILE_REPORT: exposure under $1,000, no shared device, region cluster or other customer. Not BLOCK_ALL_CARDS (R10: only one card shows fraud).
- Stop reason: verification response settles it; with a denial p rises above 0.85 on history + score + customer. No further graph step would change the actions.

## Confidence and open questions
- Medium. Could flip to legitimate if the customer confirms (amount and merchant type look like the account's normal spend, so a confirmation is plausible). Episode size is the main uncertainty: the aggregated card makes the true episode boundaries fuzzy.

## Generalizable signal
- Repeat-compromise detector: for the flagged txn's holder_key (card1|addr1|D1-derived start day), check whether any earlier txn of that holder_key is in a confirmed-fraud closed case. If yes and risk >= 0.4, treat as likely fraud (historical 86-93% fraud rate) even with no amount, region or device anomaly; if no prior fraud and risk >= 0.7, the historical fraud rate is only about 22%, so default to verify/clear. Cleared cases almost never come from prior-fraud holders.
