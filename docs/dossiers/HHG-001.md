## Recommended handling
- Evidence request: none needed. The card's own history settles it.
- Initial actions: ALLOW_TRANSACTION (auto), CLOSE_NO_FRAUD (auto, R1: score is the only signal and probability is far below 0.70, and history contradicts it). Final = initial.
- Case only vs case + SAR: neither. Probability below 0.30, no dispute, no evidence request, so no CREATE_CASE is required by 3a; GENERATE_REPORT (auto) optional for the record. No SAR.
- Stop reason: probability at or below 0.15 supported by two or more independent evidence groups (same-merchant weekly recurring charge in the sub-account's home region; no network anomaly; clean labelled history for the holder_key). Further steps would not change the decision.

## Generalizable signal
- **Holder continuity beats score.** Before acting on a score, rebuild the real sub-account (holder_key = card1|addr1|ts_day - D1). If that holder_key already has clean history (no closed-fraud txns) in the same region and the flagged txn continues its amount/cadence, fraud is about 0.1% in the labelled months. Auto-close with CLOSE_NO_FRAUD.
- **Recurring detector:** same holder_key, same ProductCD and addr1, amount within 2%, gaps of 6 to 8 days (weekly) or 28 to 32 days (monthly) means a recurring charge. A high score on one instance of it is noise.
