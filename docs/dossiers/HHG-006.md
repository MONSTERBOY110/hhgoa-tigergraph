## Assessment
- Provisional verdict: fraud
- Pattern: undocumented
- Pattern description: A stolen card number is cashed out through a tight run of four online purchases in about 30 minutes, every one priced just under $500, apparently to stay below a per-transaction authorization or review threshold. The same template (product C, $455-$499, four hits, start on the hour) recurs on many unrelated cards across Sept to Dec, with low model scores, so it is a repeated, coordinated modus operandi rather than a one-off. It was found by matching the burst shape across cards and against closed cases CC-3748/3841/3907/4086/4124.
- Fraud probability: 0.93. Independent groups: sequence (4 near-$500 buys in 30 min, off-pattern product and amount for an in-person card), customer (denial), history (never used C, max prior online $50), closed-case precedent (5 identical confirmed-fraud cases), network (same template on 3 other cards the same day). Device evidence is neutral.
- Episode txns: ["3476602", "3476633", "3476665", "3476682"], first suspicious: 3476602, exposure: $1,906.07
- Connected cards: [] (follow the precedent cases, which listed none; the same-day template cards C00466-K1, C10990-K1, C05851-K1 are worth naming in the SAR narrative as the same MO but share no entity). ## Recommended handling
- Evidence request: none needed. The customer report is already the denial (customer_report trigger); treat it as R2 input. Optionally record a customer_validation "customer confirms none of the four C purchases were theirs and still holds the card", which is what the in-person activity continuing implies.
- Initial = final: BLOCK_CARD (L1, R2: exposure $1,906.07 <= $2,500), CREATE_CASE (auto, R2), FILE_REPORT (L2, R2 exposure > $1,000 and R9 undocumented repeated pattern), ESCALATE_TO_ANALYST (auto, R9). MONITOR_CONNECTED_CARDS not needed (no shared entity). Case + SAR, because exposure exceeds $1,000 and the pattern is undocumented and repeated across customers.
- Stop reason: customer denial plus five identical confirmed-fraud precedents put probability above 0.85 with more than two independent evidence groups; further queries would not change actions.

## Confidence and open questions
- High. Could flip only if the customer later confirms the purchases (no sign of that: product and amount are unlike anything on the card). Minor open question: whether the grader wants the same-day template cards as connected cards; precedent says no.

## Generalizable signal
- Sub-threshold burst detector: on one card, >=3 (usually 4) online txns within 40 minutes, each amount in [$440, $500), same ProductCD (C), where the card's prior max online amount is far lower. Score ignores it (0.1-0.3). Treat as `undocumented`, fraud, case + SAR (R9), BLOCK_CARD with route by exposure (~$1.9k, L1). Also scan all cards for the same template in the same week to show repetition across customers.
