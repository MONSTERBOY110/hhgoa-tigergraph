## Confidence and open questions
- High on fraud, medium on the ring's exact membership. The flagged row's id_19/id_20 (417/595) and dist2 (42) differ from the six edge rows (567/507, 29). The link rests on email, device family, the D4/D15=21 and C13=0 fingerprint, the fixed amount and the counter sequence, not on one identical key. $59.6x is also a common ProductCD C price (many unrelated txns land there), so amount alone is no evidence.
- Could flip only if the customer later recognises the purchase (e.g. a household member on a new PC), which the ring evidence makes unlikely.

## Generalizable signal
- Fixed-amount ring detector: in a sliding 7-day window, group online txns by (ProductCD, amount rounded to $0.25, P_emaildomain) and keep groups spanning 4 or more distinct cards whose rows also share a stable fingerprint (same D4/D15 value with D1=0, C13=0, same device family). Treat a counter (C1/C2) that climbs across different cards as the signature of one actor. Flag every member card, even when each row fits its own card's history and scores low.
