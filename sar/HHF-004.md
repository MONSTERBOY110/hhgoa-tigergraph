## 4. Narrative

This Suspicious Activity Report concerns unauthorized activity conducted on visa debit card C12975-K2, which belongs to customer C12975.[^1] Between 2017-01-05 and 2017-01-26, an unauthorized party initiated 6 fraudulent transactions totaling $23.99 on the customer's card.[^2] The activity was conducted through an online channel under product code C, originating from billing regions 191 and 337.[^3] The initial five transactions occurred on 2017-01-05 at 11:33 for $3.51 (transaction 3660255), 2017-01-07 at 10:03 for $5.18 (transaction 3660256), 2017-01-14 at 14:57 for $2.66 (transaction 3660257), 2017-01-18 at 14:42 for $4.75 (transaction 3660258), and 2017-01-22 at 13:22 for $4.99 (transaction 3660259).[^4] Device data was captured exclusively for transaction 3660259 as rv:11.0 | | ie 11.0 for desktop |, while all other transactions had no device recorded.[^5] The final transaction, flagged transaction 3660260, was executed on 2017-01-26 at 15:09 for $2.90 in billing region 337.[^6] Regarding transaction 3660260, the cardholder reported the disputed transaction to the bank and denied making it.[^7] The unauthorized scheme was carried out using small online charges of a few dollars spread over several weeks that were never followed by a larger purchase.[^8] This activity matches none of the documented fraud typologies.[^9] The activity is considered suspicious because the same slow micro-charge pattern appeared across 7 other customer cards during the same timeframe, specifically cards C00348-K1, C01525-K2, C02851-K2, C05083-K1, C06139-K2, C09471-K2, and C12982-K1.[^10] Additionally, card C12975-K2 was previously associated with confirmed-fraud closed case CC-4890.[^11]
