## 4. Narrative

This report concerns customer C01727, card C01727-K2, issued as a visa debit.[^1] Between 2017-01-17 and 2017-01-28, 3 transaction(s) totalling $967.68 were identified as part of one fraud episode, beginning with transaction 3660261.[^2] The activity took place online, product code(s) C, billing region(s) 231.[^3] The transactions were purchases paying one recipient email domain that suddenly drew payments from several unrelated customers' cards.[^4] The activity is assessed as an undocumented coordinated pattern: R_emaildomain sc.rr.com was paid by 4 cards in the 1 day up to 2017-01-28 against 35 card-days in the previous 56 days; Card C01727-K2 already has 5 confirmed-fraud closed case. The account key is not usable in January, so the precedent is read at card level, where it is weaker: on cards of this size an earlier confirmed case made a new alert about 3.9 times as likely to be fraud.[^5] The shared element (recipient email domain sc.rr.com) links this activity to 16 other card(s), including C00534-K2, C01357-K1, C01507-K1, C01594-K1, which indicates a common actor across cardholders.[^6] Earlier confirmed cases CC-0723, CC-1833, CC-1501 show the same pattern.[^7] The cardholder has not yet been contacted.[^8] The bank has recommended BLOCK_CARD, MONITOR_CONNECTED_CARDS and opened an internal case; the report is filed under R9, §3a.[^9]
