## 4. Narrative

This Suspicious Activity Report relates to unauthorized card activity identified on customer C11663 under case HHF-003.[^1] Between January 22, 2017, and January 31, 2017, seven unauthorized online transactions totaling $8,551.47 were conducted on the customer's mastercard debit card C11663-K1.[^2] The activity commenced on January 22, 2017, at 12:55 with transaction 3660147 for $181.73 in billing region 327 under product code C, with no device recorded.[^3] Subsequent transactions in billing region 327 under product code C included transaction 3660148 for $357.82 on January 23, 2017, at 07:25, transaction 3660149 for $811.92 on January 24, 2017, at 19:13, and three consecutive $1,800.00 purchases numbered 3660150, 3660151, and 3660152 occurring on January 26, 27, and 28, 2017, respectively.[^4] The final flagged transaction, 3660153, was initiated on January 31, 2017, at 17:31:28 for $1,800.00 under product code C in billing region 315.[^5] The cardholder reported the disputed transaction to the bank and denied making it.[^6] The unauthorized activity was carried out through online purchases of one product stepping up roughly doubling in amount until reaching a fixed cap of $1,800.00.[^7] This pattern matches none of the documented fraud typologies.[^8] The activity is suspicious because the identical amount ladder capped at $1,800.00 was also observed across connected cards C08563-K1 and C08943-K2 belonging to other customers within 14 days, indicating a coordinated multi-account cash-out probing transaction limits.[^9] In response, the bank blocked card C11663-K1 and placed the connected cards on monitoring.[^10]
