## The seven scoring items

| Item | What Kavach does | Command | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vector search in decisions** | 384-d embeddings (bge-small, local) of every closed-case analyst note (`ClosedCase.emb`), every policy and pattern section (`PolicyDoc.emb`) and every written-back investigation summary (`InvestigationCase.emb`), searched with `vectorSearch()` in the installed queries `similar_closed`, `similar_docs`, `similar_investigations`. The results fill `similar_prior_cases` and pick the policy section cited for the decisive rule. Investigation summaries are embedded on write-back, but nearest-neighbour claims over them are not cited as evidence: summaries are re-embedded whenever a case is re-run, so such a claim could not be re-checked later; case memory cites earlier investigations by graph traversal (`case_memory` by card) instead. Verdicts and actions stay deterministic by design (the brief's least-privilege rule): vector results are attested evidence, not a vote. | `python -m kavach investigate HHF-006 --backend tg` | 5,585 + 19 + 18 vectors on the graph; self-check (a note's nearest neighbour is itself) passes |
| **Vector search fused with traversal** | `fused_precedent` (GSQL, `kavach/graph/queries_fusion.gsql`): from the case's card to its transactions in the 30 days to the alert, to the rare elements they carry (a handset-level device profile on 25 or fewer cards, a recipient email domain or billing region used by 25 or fewer cards in the window), to the other cards using them, to every ClosedCase on or connected to those cards (`ON_CARD`, `CONNECTED_TO`); then `vectorSearch({ClosedCase.emb}, qv, k, {candidate_set: X})` ranks exactly that set. Each hit carries its graph path (own card, connected card, or which shared element and through which card). | `python -m kavach.graph.fusion precedent HHF-006 --backend tg` ; study: `python logs/tgdepth/study.py` | Fused top 5 differs from plain vector search on **13 of 13** cases that have connected precedent and from plain traversal on **9 of 13**. On legitimate HHF-014 and HHF-016, traversal alone ranks five confirmed frauds first; fused ranks five cleared cases first. |
| **A graph algorithm that affects output** | `ring_wcc` (GSQL): weakly connected components by label propagation over the card graph whose edges are rare elements shared by the cards' **atypical** transactions in the window (a product, an amount or a proxy the card never showed before the window), plus the ring template a typology detector matched. | **Case write-back and read-back** | `kavach/persist.py`: InvestigationCase plus `CASE_OF`, `CASE_CARD`, `CASE_TXN`, `CASE_DEVICE`, `CASE_SIMILAR`; a re-run replaces the decision links; links use `vertex_must_exist`; `written_to_graph` only after the vertex and every link count re-read. `kavach/graph/writeback.py`: every casefile event as a CaseEvent (`CASE_EVENT`), every approval as an Approval (insert-only, `CASE_APPROVAL`), the L2-approved grown detector as a Detector (`APPROVED_DETECTOR` from its approval, `DETECTOR_HIT` to the 36 transactions it fires on, `CASE_DETECTOR` from HHF-006). | **Restricted MCP tools** | `--backend mcp` runs every tool through the TigerGraph MCP server. The only MCP tool ever called is `tigergraph__run_installed_query`, and only for installed queries defined in our own read-only GSQL files (`kavach/graph/mcp_client.py`, allowlist enforced before the call). No GSQL, upsert or schema tool is reachable from the agent; the LLM never sees a tool. | `python -m kavach investigate HHF-006 --backend mcp` | 41 queries on the allowlist (45 with `queries_fusion.gsql`); a non-listed query raises PermissionError before any call. fused_precedent and ring_wcc through MCP: same ids as DuckDB on HHF-006 (3.3 s, 1.0 s) |
| **The graph as source of truth** | On `--backend tg` every investigation tool (transactions, cards, devices, emails, regions, holders, closed cases, typology mirrors, grown-detector tools, case memory, fused precedent, ring components) is an installed query; there is no fallback to DuckDB: a graph failure is an incident (fail closed). ## Guarantees and limits, stated plainly
- Insert-only history: CaseEvent and Approval are written with `new_vertex_only`; a vertex already on the graph with another hash stops the sync with an error and nothing is overwritten. The chain hash is the local file's; the graph copy can be checked against it at any time (`python -m kavach.graph.writeback verify`).
- One TigerGraph quirk handled: a query result containing the text "Runtime Error:" comes back as HTTP 400 with a correct body, so the graph copy of free text writes that marker with a dash (`docs/tigergraph-feedback.md`).
- The ring algorithm confirms rather than changes the six ring answers today (it adds 0 cards to their connected cards; the typology detectors already found them); its new information is the HHF-006 recovery without any detector and the HHF-013 card's membership of the same component, which the lead reviews.
- Vector search informs evidence and precedent, never the verdict: a probability moved by a similarity score could not be calibrated or attested.
