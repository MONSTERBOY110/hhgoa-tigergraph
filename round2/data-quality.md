Consequence of each flag:

- `flagged_txn_missing`: The flagged transaction is not in the index; the case cannot be investigated and must fail closed.
- `alert_card_mismatch`: The alert names a card the flagged transaction is not on; the investigation follows the transaction's own card, and anything said about the stated card would describe another account.
- `no_identity_record`: No device or browser record for the flagged transaction: device, new-device and proxy signals cannot fire either way, and the absence of device evidence is not evidence of innocence.
- `empty_billing_region`: No billing region on the flagged transaction: region checks (out-of-region use, region clusters, holder_key) cannot run.
- `thin_history`: Fewer than 5 prior transactions on the card and on its holder key: amount, product, region and device novelty have no baseline, so profile-fit signals are unreliable.
- `placeholder_device`: The device profile is a generic value shared by many customers; it can neither link cards nor separate them.
- `january_score`: January risk_score comes from a model that was not retrained: it is clamped, a low score is not evidence of innocence and a high score alone cannot carry a verdict.

Rule for the agent (TRD2 section 9): when the flagged transaction has no identity record and the account has fewer than 5 prior transactions, p is capped at 0.60 unless a network or history group supports fraud, and the summary says why.
