# Typology backtests (S2)

Generated by `python -m kavach.backtest all` (kavach/backtest.py) from the closed-case history (5,585 cases) and a reference sample of non-fraud transactions before January 2017. Thresholds are chosen on a small grid by the highest lift with cleared hits <= 3 and support >= 15; a rule is `validated` when that lift is >= 3, and its runtime LLR is min(2.0, 0.5 ln lift). Anything else is `weak`: LLR min(0.5, 0.5 ln lift), and 0 when the rule never fires on confirmed fraud. The per-hit transaction and card ids are in `detectors/backtests/<name>.json`.

### `common_origin`

## Cross-customer rings: where a backtest cannot exist, and the stated weight

Three January shapes repeat across customers but never occur in the labelled months, so the closed cases hold no example to fit a likelihood ratio on (0 confirmed-fraud hits, 0 cleared hits, 0 hits in the 200,000-transaction reference sample):

| ring | single-card form | cross-customer form | labelled months | January |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `proxy_new_burst_ring` | 2 or more purchases of $200 or more of one product within 72 hours, every one from a device marked New behind an anonymous proxy | the same template on at least 2 other cards within 14 days, no shared device | 0 / 200,000 | HHF-001, HHF-002 (14 other cards each) |
| `amount_ladder_ring` | 4 or more online purchases of one product, 3 or more steps of 1.5x or more, top of $1,000 or more, within 14 days | the same ladder top (within 1%) on at least 2 other cards within 14 days | 0 / 200,000 | HHF-003 (cap $1,800.00, 2 other cards) |
| `micro_charges_ring` | 3 or more online charges under $10 within 30 days, no larger purchase within an hour, no micro habit (validated: lift 3.60, 18 fraud, 0 cleared) | 5 or more charges under $6 in 30 days on at least 2 other no-habit cards, at 3 times the average of the previous three 30-day windows | weekly scan October to December: never fires | HHF-004 (7 other cards), HHF-005 (11) |

For these the runtime weight is a **stated** coordination weight of 1.5 (the same weight Round 1 used for template rings), applied only when the template repeats on the case card plus at least 2 other customers' cards, and every claim says so in its text. A ring also makes the case R9 (coordinated activity fitting none of the five documented patterns): the other cards become connected cards, so the case files a report and is escalated. The profile-fit signals a pattern mimics by design (amount within the account's range, usual hour, product already used) are not counted as alibis when a pattern episode explains the fit.

Bug found while integrating: `micro_rule` treated a generic browser profile ("rv:11.0 | | ie 11.0 for desktop |", on 480 cards) on one micro charge as a device identity, so the cardholder's own later purchase on the same browser family vetoed the rule. Generic profiles are now excluded (`network.generic_device`); after the fix the single-card rule validates (it was weak before).
